HUMAN LABEL AUDIT -- COHEN'S KAPPA REPORT (FINAL, n=40)
======================================================================
Methodology: 40-ward stratified sample (10 each from rule:formal,
rule:informal, thana:informal_anchor, thana:formal_anchor), independently
visually classified from Google Maps satellite imagery by the author,
blind to the rule/anchor label. Judgment made per-ward in isolation
(not by comparison against other wards). One ward (Kalindi) was dropped
and replaced with a fresh, never-reviewed ward after its original
judgment was potentially compromised by an answer being named in the
course of discussion; its replacement was rated with no information
about the correct answer.

RESULT
------
n                 : 40
Raw agreement     : 0.725 (72.5%)
Cohen's kappa     : 0.450  -- "moderate" agreement (Landis & Koch 1977 scale)

Confusion matrix (rows=human judgment, cols=rule/anchor label):
                  rule=informal   rule=formal
human=informal               16             7
human=formal                  4            13

INTERPRETATION
--------------
Moderate agreement (kappa=0.45) between independent visual judgment and
the rule/anchor labeling pipeline. This is weaker than "substantial"
but clearly better than chance, and supports the labels as a reasonable
(not perfect) ground truth. The residual disagreement is informative in
itself: of 11 total disagreements, slightly more are
human=informal/rule=formal (7) than the reverse (4), suggesting a mild
tendency for the rule/anchor-formal class to include some
visually-ambiguous or peri-urban wards. This should be reported in the
manuscript as an honest limitation alongside the headline validation
results (external EO4SD correlation, spatial CV, ablation), not
presented as a stronger result than it is.
